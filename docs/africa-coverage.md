# Africa coverage review — 2026-09-22

46 World Heritage properties, 15 thematic chapters, 23 present-day country labels, and 15 component visiting stops. Component stops are not separate inscriptions. Source identity is the UNESCO property number; each entry links to its official page.

The collection covers human origins, rock art, Nile states, Kush, ancient Mediterranean cities, Christian traditions, Islamic cities, Saharan trade, Swahili ports, southern African states, living landscapes, indigenous kingdoms, maritime rivalry and slavery, colonial modernism, and resistance to apartheid. These are overlapping regional themes, not universal stages.

Egypt's six cultural inscriptions are included. Saint Catherine is grouped by present-day country (Egypt), although Sinai is geographically in Asia. Natural-only inscriptions are outside this historical selection. This is not a complete African World Heritage inventory. Central Africa, independence movements beyond South Africa, and many countries remain underrepresented.

`dist/africa-records.js` is the authoritative African data source. World views, country filters, details, chapter links, journal validation and public export consume it. Existing record keys and UNESCO IDs are preserved. Source-linked dates refer to historical periods, not inscription years. Visiting questions are editorial prompts, not official UNESCO text.
